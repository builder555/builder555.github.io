A.G. Riddle

New York Times Bestselling Author

A.G. Riddle spent ten years building and selling internet companies before retiring to pursue his first love: writing fiction. He has sold over five million copies worldwide, with his books translated into two dozen languages.

His novels explore the intersection of science, history, and human nature — weaving together ancient mysteries, near-future technology, and the timeless question of what it means to be human. Every story begins with a single obsessive question that won't let go.
